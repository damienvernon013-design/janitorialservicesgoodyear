Janitorial Services in Verrado, AZ

Verrado is approximately 8 miles west of our Goodyear base — about 12 minutes by our standard crew routing. We provide nightly janitorial service, day porter coverage and floor stripping and waxing to commercial facilities throughout Verrado.

About the Verrado Commercial Market

Verrado is a master-planned community within Buckeye's city limits, developed by DMB Associates starting in the early 2000s. Its commercial district — Main Street Verrado — is a traditional neighborhood design (TND) retail and office corridor within walking distance of the residential core, unusual for a West Valley community of its size.

Main Street Verrado's ground-floor retail and second-floor professional office stock serves an affluent residential catchment — the commercial tenants here include medical, dental, financial and legal practices with above-average front-of-house appearance standards.

Verrado's design standards for commercial properties include specific signage and exterior maintenance requirements enforced by a commercial property association, which extends expectations about interior cleanliness — facilities managers here tend to be more specification-driven than average.
