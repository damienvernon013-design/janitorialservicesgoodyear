Janitorial Services in Avondale, AZ

Avondale is approximately 5 miles east of our Goodyear base — about 10 minutes by our standard crew routing. We provide nightly janitorial service, day porter coverage and floor stripping and waxing to commercial facilities throughout Avondale.

About the Avondale Commercial Market

Avondale's primary commercial corridor runs along Van Buren Street and Indian School Road, with a mix of light industrial, retail and office stock that has densified significantly since the city annexed additional unincorporated Maricopa County land in the early 2000s.

The Avondale Business Center on Dysart Road anchors a cluster of professional office and flex-industrial tenants, including several healthcare-adjacent businesses that require disinfection-grade cleaning protocols.

Phoenix Raceway sits within Avondale's city limits — during NASCAR and IndyCar race weeks, commercial facilities near Cotton Lane and I-10 handle elevated staffing that increases restroom and common-area service demand.
