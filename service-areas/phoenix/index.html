Janitorial Services in Phoenix, AZ

Phoenix is approximately 22 miles east of our Goodyear base — about 25 minutes by our standard crew routing. We provide nightly janitorial service, day porter coverage and floor stripping and waxing to commercial facilities throughout Phoenix.

About the Phoenix Commercial Market

The Phoenix accounts we service are concentrated on the west side — the area west of I-17, including the Estrella, Laveen and Maryvale commercial corridors — which are within our 25-mile service radius from Goodyear. Midtown and downtown Phoenix accounts fall outside our practical service range.

The Laveen Village area along Dobbins Road has seen significant commercial development in the past decade as southwest Phoenix's residential base expanded — medical offices, financial services and retail-adjacent professional businesses now populate corridors that were largely vacant agricultural land before 2010.

West Phoenix industrial accounts near the 27th Avenue and Buckeye Road corridors include food-processing and packaging facilities that have documentation requirements similar to Tolleson's produce-handling sector — service logs and disinfection records are a standard expectation for facilities in these corridors.
