Our Janitorial Services in Goodyear

We provide three core commercial janitorial services to facilities in Goodyear and the West Valley. All three can be quoted as stand-alone services or combined into a single scope and invoice.

Nightly Janitorial Service

After-hours cleaning for offices, warehouses, distribution centers and manufacturing facilities. Five-night or seven-night schedules. Scope written before the first crew visit.

Day Porter & Restroom Service

Daytime staffing for restroom maintenance, common area upkeep and trash management during operational hours. Quoted per hour with minimum engagement.

Floor Stripping & Waxing

Strip and recoat VCT and resilient flooring. Scheduled off-hours to avoid operational disruption. Quoted per square foot of treatable surface.

How We Scope and Price

Every service starts with a facility walkthrough. After the walkthrough we produce a written scope of work listing every task, frequency and method. You review the scope before we quote a price. Nothing is verbal. If it is not in the scope, it is not in the price.

Services can be bundled: a nightly janitorial contract with a day porter add-on is common for larger facilities with high foot traffic during the day. Floor work is typically on a quarterly or semi-annual schedule added to an existing contract.
