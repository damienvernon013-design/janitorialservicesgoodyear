What's Included in Nightly Janitorial Service?

What's in your quote varies by provider — know exactly what's covered before you sign anything.

Not all nightly janitorial contracts cover the same tasks, and what is included in your quote varies between providers. Before you sign anything, you should know exactly what is — and is not — part of your standard service.

Standard Inclusions

  • Vacuuming all carpeted areas, including under desks and in corners
  • Mopping hard floors — VCT, tile, sealed concrete
  • Trash and recycling removal, bins relined
  • Restroom cleaning — toilets, sinks, mirrors, floors scrubbed and disinfected; consumables restocked
  • Break room surface cleaning — counters, sink, microwave exterior
  • Surface dusting — desks, shelves, window ledges
  • Interior glass and mirror cleaning
  • Lobby and entry area cleaning

Common Add-Ons, Usually Priced Separately

  • Carpet extraction or steam cleaning
  • Floor stripping, waxing and buffing
  • Exterior window washing
  • High dusting — vents, ceiling fans, top of shelving
  • Consumable restocking package (paper towels, soap, tissue)

Ask for the Task List, Not a Verbal Summary

Request a written scope of work rather than a verbal description. It protects you if quality falls short and gives you a clear basis for evaluating a crew's performance over time.

Every Facility Gets Customized

If you have a server room that should never be entered, a specific floor-care schedule, or a break room with particular equipment, tell us before the walkthrough — your written scope should reflect your actual facility, not a generic template.
