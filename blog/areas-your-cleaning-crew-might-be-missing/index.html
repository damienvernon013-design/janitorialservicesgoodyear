Areas Your Cleaning Crew Might Be Missing

Even facilities that look clean often harbor a handful of neglected spots.

Even offices that look clean often harbor a handful of neglected spots that accumulate dust, grime and bacteria over time. If your current cleaning crew is not addressing these, you are paying for a partial clean.

1. Keyboards and Mice

Studies have found keyboards carry surprisingly high bacterial loads compared to other office surfaces, yet most standard cleaning routines skip them entirely without explicit instructions.

2. Light Switches and Outlet Plates

High-touch surfaces that almost never get wiped in a standard routine.

3. Door Handles and Push Plates

Touched constantly throughout the day — among the highest pathogen-transfer points in any commercial building.

4. Tops of Filing Cabinets and Tall Shelving

Out of sight until someone reaches up for something. High dusting requires extension tools and deliberate effort most routine passes skip.

5. Break Room Appliance Handles

Refrigerator handles and microwave buttons see constant use but often only the exterior surfaces get wiped.

6. Air Vents and Return Grilles

Dusty vents redistribute allergens and dust directly into breathing zones — relevant year-round in a dusty desert climate. These should be wiped monthly and deep-cleaned quarterly.

7. Chair Bases and Casters

Rolling chair wheels pick up debris from every floor surface and spread it. Chair bases should be cleaned monthly.

8. Phone Handsets

Used near the mouth multiple times daily, office phones are a significant surface that rarely appears on a standard checklist.

9. Blinds and Window Tracks

Blinds accumulate dust quickly and release it back into the air whenever adjusted.

10. Under and Behind Furniture

Dust and debris accumulate under desks and behind furniture without periodic furniture-moving during deeper cleans.
