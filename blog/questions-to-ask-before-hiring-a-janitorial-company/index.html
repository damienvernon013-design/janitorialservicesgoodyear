Questions to Ask Before Hiring a Janitorial Company

A few good questions during the evaluation process can save months of frustration.

Choosing a janitorial company is a relationship you will likely live with for a year or more. A few pointed questions during evaluation can save months of frustration later.

  1. How long have you been in business, and do you have references from similar facilities? Relevant experience matters more than longevity alone.
  2. Are your employees background-checked? Crews work in your building, often outside business hours — this should be non-negotiable.
  3. Are you bonded and insured? Ask for a current certificate of insurance covering general liability and workers' compensation.
  4. What products and equipment do you use? Product and equipment choices say a lot about a company's standards.
  5. Who will actually clean my building? Know whether staff are employees or subcontractors.
  6. What happens if I'm not satisfied with a clean? Ask about their complaint process and response time.
  7. Do you provide a written scope of work? Any provider unwilling to put details in writing is a red flag.
  8. What are your contract terms and cancellation policy? Understand commitment length and auto-renewal terms upfront.
  9. Do you have experience with my type of facility? Medical, industrial and office facilities each have different requirements.
  10. How do you train and supervise staff? Training and supervision determine whether standards hold up over time.

Ask us any of these directly during your quote request — we would rather answer them upfront than have you find out the hard way later.
