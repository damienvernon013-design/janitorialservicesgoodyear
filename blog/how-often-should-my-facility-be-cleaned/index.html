How Often Should Your Facility Be Cleaned?

Getting frequency right means you're not overpaying for service you don't need — or undercleaning until it shows.

This is one of the most common questions we get from facility managers shopping for janitorial service. The right answer depends on a handful of factors specific to your space — getting it right means you are not overpaying for visits you do not need, or letting things slide until it shows.

The General Range

For most office environments, two to three visits per week balances hygiene, cost and necessity. Higher-traffic facilities — more than roughly 20 employees, client-facing spaces, or production areas — typically need nightly service.

What Determines Frequency

  • Headcount — more people means more mess and more high-touch surface use.
  • Client or visitor traffic — spaces the public regularly enters usually justify nightly service.
  • Industry — medical and food-adjacent facilities often require daily or multiple-daily attention by their own standards.
  • Layout — open-plan spaces with shared surfaces accumulate grime faster than private offices.
  • Season — desert dust and monsoon-season debris can justify temporarily increasing frequency in the Goodyear area.

By Facility Type

Small professional office: one to two visits weekly. Mid-size office: two to three visits weekly. Large open-plan or distribution facility: nightly. Medical or dental office: nightly, with additional sanitation protocols.

Don't Skip Periodic Deep Cleans

Regardless of your regular frequency, schedule periodic deep cleans for carpets, floor finish and hard-to-reach areas. These are typically an add-on to an existing nightly or day-porter contract, not a separate relationship.
