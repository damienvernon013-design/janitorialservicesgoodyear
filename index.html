What We Do in Goodyear

Goodyear's industrial base runs heavily toward distribution and light manufacturing — the Loop 303 corridor from Palm Valley Road to Sarival Avenue carries some of the densest warehouse square footage in the West Valley. That mix means most of our Goodyear accounts need nightly janitorial coverage for break rooms, locker areas and offices, day porter work for restrooms during production hours, and periodic floor maintenance on VCT and sealed concrete. We quote all three as a combined scope when it makes financial sense to do so.
